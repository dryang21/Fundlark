# Fundlark v4.1 — explainable eligibility screening

Fundlark continues to use two distinct concepts:

1. **Research relevance** decides whether the opportunity belongs in the personalized Step 2 list. The Step 3 exploration index is intentionally profile-independent.
2. **Applicant eligibility** produces an *assessment* for that opportunity from explicitly indexed requirements and the researcher's locally entered information.

## Three classification labels

- **Likely eligible**: a recently reviewed record explicitly lists an individual submission route, the researcher's career stage, and at least one structured eligibility rule; all indexed conditions match the supplied profile and no checks remain unresolved. This is an assessment of *known* requirements only, **not eligibility approval**. Records with stage or route information alone are insufficient.
- **Needs verification**: the source data is incomplete, sponsor access must be confirmed, the listed application round is uncertain, the record is stale/marked for review, or profile information required to evaluate a condition is missing. Missing information is never treated as a rejection.
- **Likely ineligible**: at least one mandatory *indexed* requirement conflicts with a supplied profile fact (career stage, required research effort, citizenship category, PI privileges, specific host restrictions, career window or appointment), subject to a possible faculty/host route where explicitly indicated. This is not a final sponsor decision.

## What the applicant sees

Step 2 dashboard now shows status counts and an **Eligibility assessment** dropdown with All statuses / Likely eligible / Needs verification / Likely ineligible, alongside the existing funder-source and other filters. Every visible personalized funding row displays a status badge. Expand **Eligibility, program details and source status** to see passed criteria (✓), unresolved criteria (?) and known conflicts (×), a suggested next step and the source-review date. Results with known blockers remain discoverable unless research relevance itself is insufficient. Priority recommendations omit known ineligible records.

Step 1 includes a stage-appropriate optional **required mentor / sponsor confirmed** field. Unconfirmed membership, protected research time, institution, citizenship status, PI rights or mentor support are not inferred from the applicant's CV. An unknown citizenship selection does not imply a person's nationality or immigration status.

## Data-coverage limits

The bundled catalog is unchanged and not exhaustively source-verified. Many records have only free-text eligibility descriptions and no sufficiently complete structured rules. They will correctly remain **Needs verification**, even if their topics match exceptionally well. A currently closed round is not marked likely eligible solely from historical requirements. The new classifier does not browse sponsor sites or approve an application; consult the official current call.

Maintain funding eligibility in `docs/demo/eligibility.mjs`. Recommended structured fields in `eligibility_rules` include `application_route`, `membership_required`, `institution_required`, `minimum_research_effort_percent`, `pi_privileges_required`, `citizenship_required`, `host_regions`, `mentor_required`, `minimum_years_since_phd`, `max_years_since_phd`, `eligible_appointments`, and `eligible_faculty_ranks`. `last_verified_at` signals record review, not that the funder certifies a user's eligibility. Rules not encoded in fields **cannot** be checked by this classifier.

## Regression coverage

`node --test tests-js/eligibility-classification.test.mjs` covers the three statuses, incomplete records, protected research time, sponsor routes, citizenship and PI restrictions, academic career stages, source staleness, and Step 2 filtering. Run `npm run test:core` and `pytest -q` for the rest of the suite.
